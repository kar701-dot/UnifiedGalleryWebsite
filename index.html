Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. This is a simple one-time purchase for the Pro version. You own your license key permanently with no hidden recurring costs.

Does the app upload my photos?

No. Your media strictly remains inside your own direct OneDrive or Dropbox accounts. Indexing happens exclusively locally on your machine.

Which cloud providers are supported?

Currently, the application fully supports OneDrive and Dropbox integrations. Support for additional cloud storage engines is planned in upcoming roadmap updates.

Is an internet connection required?

An internet connection is needed to retrieve and fetch cloud sync data, but browsing previously indexed catalogs is fully optimized for offline local operations.
